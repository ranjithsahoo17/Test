On the page's performOk / Apply handler: for every row where isModifiedAgainstBaseline() is true, reject if teReference is empty (REQ 3), showing the error against that row. Then build the deviation payloads from the diff and send them.

Steps 9+ — server side

New JPA entities, the two @Stateless services, and the ThresholdResolver keyed on session data date. These block on the answer to the question from earlier: remote EJB over JNDI, or JAX-RS? That decides whether adding a service method means regenerating client stubs and redeploying the EAR.

Suggested commit order

Steps 1, 2, 3 land together and compile cleanly on their own. Step 4 is where the build breaks if the positional arrays are wrong — do it as its own commit so a bisect finds it. Steps 5 and 6 make the columns actually work. Everything through step 6 is testable with a hardcoded setDeviated(true) on one row and no server at all — worth doing before touching GlassFish.
